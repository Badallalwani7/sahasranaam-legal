# Privacy Policy: Shri Vishnu Sahasranaam

_Last updated: 15 October 2026_

This policy explains what information the **Shri Vishnu Sahasranaam** app ("the app",
"we") handles, why, and the choices you have. Contact: **badallalwani9@gmail.com**.

## Summary
- Your chanting history, streak, name and settings are **stored only on your device**.
- If you choose to sign in, your account (name, email, account ID) is managed by Google
  Firebase Authentication.
- We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used, and Google AdMob to
  show ads to users who are not Premium.
- We do **not** sell your personal information.

## Information stored only on your device
Your name (as you type it in the app), language, Sadhana time, reminder preferences,
chanting sessions, streaks and practice statistics are saved locally on your phone. They
are not sent to us. You can erase them anytime in **Settings → Clear Progress**, or by
uninstalling the app. If your phone's system backup is enabled, your device may include
this data in your personal backup (e.g. Google One or iCloud), under that provider's terms.

## Information collected by services we use

**Account (optional): Firebase Authentication, by Google.**
If you sign in with Google (or, on iPhone, with Apple), we receive your name, email address
and an account identifier, used only to provide your account. On Android you can instead
continue as a guest, which creates an anonymous identifier.

**Analytics: Google Analytics for Firebase.**
Collects app interactions (for example, screens opened and sessions completed), a device
or app-instance identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and
diagnostic information, to help us improve the app.

**Advertising: Google AdMob.**
Shows banner and optional rewarded ads to non-Premium users. AdMob may collect your
device's advertising identifier, approximate location, app interactions and diagnostics
to serve and measure ads. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), we ask
for your consent first through Google's consent form. On iPhone we do not request
tracking permission, so ads are not personalised. You can reset or limit ad personalisation
in your device settings.

**Notifications: Firebase Cloud Messaging.**
A device token lets us deliver reminders. Most reminders are scheduled on your device.

**Purchases: Google Play Billing / Apple App Store.**
Subscriptions are processed by Google or Apple. We receive confirmation of your purchase
status only, never your payment details.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## How we use information
To provide and improve the app, keep your account working, deliver reminders you asked
for, show ads to free users, process subscriptions, and prevent abuse. We do not use your
information for any other purpose, and we do not sell it.

## Data retention and deletion
- **Account:** delete it anytime in the app: **Profile → Account → Delete account**.
- **Local data:** **Settings → Clear Progress**, or uninstall the app.
- **Analytics and advertising data** are retained by Google according to its retention
  settings. To request deletion of any data associated with you, email badallalwani9@gmail.com.

## Security
Data sent by the app is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Data stored on your device is
protected by your device's security.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and
we will delete it.

## Your rights
Depending on where you live (for example under India's DPDP Act or the EU GDPR), you may
have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to withdraw consent.
Email badallalwani9@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

## Changes
We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date and, for significant
changes, notify you in the app.
