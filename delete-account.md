# Delete your account or data: Shri Vishnu Sahasranaam

This page explains how to delete your **Shri Vishnu Sahasranaam** (Vishnu Sahasranamam: Audio)
account and data. Developer: **Badal Lalwani**. Contact: badallalwani9@gmail.com.

## Delete your account (in the app)
1. Open the app and go to the **Profile** tab.
2. In the **Account** card, tap **Delete account**.
3. Confirm by tapping **DELETE**.

Your sign-in account (name, email address and account ID held by Firebase Authentication)
is deleted immediately and permanently.

## Delete your chanting data without deleting your account
1. Open **Profile → Settings** (gear icon).
2. Under **Data & Privacy**, tap **Clear Progress** and confirm.

This erases your streaks, chanting sessions and practice history from your device.
Uninstalling the app also removes all data stored on the device.

## Request deletion by email
If you can't access the app, email **badallalwani9@gmail.com** from the address linked to
your account with the subject "Delete my data". We delete your account and any associated
data within 30 days and confirm by email.

## What is deleted and what is kept
- **Deleted:** your sign-in account (name, email, account ID) and all chanting history.
- **Kept by Google:** anonymous analytics and advertising data processed by Google
  (Firebase Analytics, AdMob) is retained under Google's own retention settings (up to 14
  months for analytics) and is not linked to your deleted account.
- **Purchases:** subscription records are kept by Google Play or the App Store as required
  for billing and tax purposes. Cancel subscriptions in your store account.
